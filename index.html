01 / THE MISSING CHECK

Authorization can outlive its reason.

Smart-account permissions, spend limits and transaction simulation answer whether an action is currently permitted. They do not preserve the causal link between a decision made at T0 and an execution attempted at T1.

COUNTERMAND turns that decision into a short-lived object: policy, T0 context, exact action, validity window and integrity digest. Immediately before execution, the old decision is tested against fresh state.

NOT

a wallet

It does not custody funds or replace account controls.

NOT

a policy engine

It can consume policy, but its job is cross-time decision validity.

NOT

another agent

The model may propose. It does not self-authorize stale reasoning.

IS

a decision boundary

A final check on whether the reason still exists before value moves.

02 / BASE-FIRST

Built for the moment agents become economic actors.

Base already gives agents the pieces needed to transact: USDC, smart accounts, spend permissions, x402 services and onchain markets. COUNTERMAND is designed to sit immediately before that execution boundary.
